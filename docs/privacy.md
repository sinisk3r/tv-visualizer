# Privacy policy: Visualizer for Android TV

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Visualizer shows animated visuals that move with the music your TV is playing.

**What it reads.** To follow the music, the app reads the sound your TV is playing through Android's audio
visualizer, the same feature other music visualizers use. Android shows this as microphone ("record audio")
access. The app does not use a microphone to listen to the room.

**What happens to it.** The sound is analysed on the TV, a fraction of a second at a time, to drive the visuals.
It is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

**Song details (optional).** The music card in the menu can show the song, artist and album art of the app
playing music, if you turn on "notification access" for Visualizer in TV Settings. The app asks only when you choose
"See song and album art". It then reads only what music apps publish as "now playing"; it does not read or keep your
notifications. The song details are shown on the TV and never stored or sent. Play, pause and skip work
without this access; moving within the song (the card's scrubber) needs it.

**Network.** The app uses the internet only when you choose "Get all presets" in the menu. It then downloads a
preset pack from GitHub (github.com/projectM-visualizer). Nothing about you or your TV is sent, beyond what any
download sends (such as your IP address, seen by GitHub).

**What it stores.** Your settings, favourites and hidden presets, and the picture size each preset runs best at,
stay on the TV in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

**No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** The app collects no personal data and shares nothing with anyone.

**Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

**Source.** The app is open source: https://github.com/sinisk3r/tv-visualizer

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/sinisk3r/tv-visualizer/issues
