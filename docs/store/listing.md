# Play Store listing (draft)

Not a trademark: avoid "MilkDrop" and "Winamp" in the title, short description and graphics. Naming the preset
format in the full description ("plays .milk presets") is factual and fine.

**App name (30 max):** Visualizer: Music Visuals for TV

**Short description (80 max):** Psychedelic visuals that dance to whatever your TV is playing. Free, no ads.

**Full description:**

Turn your TV into a light show. Visualizer draws flowing, colourful visuals that react to the music your TV is
playing, from Spotify, YouTube Music or any other app.

- 400 visuals built in, and 9,795 more in one download, from the classic "Cream of the Crop" collection of
  community-made presets.
- Follows the music closely: visuals pulse with the beat and switch on big drops and when the song changes.
- Made for the remote: left and right change the visual, up and down change the style, OK opens the menu.
- Favourites, styles and sub-styles, a hold button for the one you love, and hiding for the ones you don't.
- Smooth on everyday TVs: the picture size adapts to each visual, so motion stays fluid.
- Free and open source. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

Built on projectM, the open-source music visualizer library (LGPL-2.1). Presets by their authors, named in each
preset's title.

**Category:** Music & Audio. **Content rating:** Everyone (swear words in preset names are masked).
**Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/sinisk3r/tv-visualizer/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
**Data safety:** no data collected, no data shared. Audio is processed on the device only.
